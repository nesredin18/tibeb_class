Introduction to Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, and the fundamental forces of nature. It seeks to understand the universe at its most basic level, from the smallest subatomic particles to the largest galaxies. This chapter will introduce you to the core concepts of physics, including mechanics, thermodynamics, and electromagnetism. We will explore how these principles govern the behavior of objects and systems in our everyday lives and beyond.
